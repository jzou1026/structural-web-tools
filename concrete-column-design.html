Theory & Documentation

1. Engineering Theory

The section solver enforces plane sections remaining plane, neglects concrete tension, uses a rectangular concrete compression stress block, and models each reinforcing bar as elastic-perfectly-plastic steel. A neutral-axis angle and depth sweep generates the three-dimensional design-strength surface.

Equation 1 — section strain\[\varepsilon(x,y)=\varepsilon_{cu}\left(1-\frac{d_n(x,y)}{c}\right)\]

where \(\varepsilon\) is longitudinal strain, \(\varepsilon_{cu}\) is the concrete compression strain limit, \(d_n\) is distance from the extreme compression line toward the section, and \(c\) is neutral-axis depth.

Equation 2 — steel stress\[f_s=\operatorname{clip}(E_s\varepsilon_s,-f_y,+f_y)\]

where \(f_s\) is bar stress, \(E_s\) is steel modulus, \(\varepsilon_s\) is bar strain, and \(f_y\) is yield strength.

Equation 3 — resultants\[P_r=\sum F_i,\quad M_{xr}=\sum F_i y_i,\quad M_{yr}=-\sum F_i x_i\]

where \(F_i\) is the design force in a concrete cell or reinforcing bar and \(x_i,y_i\) are its centroid coordinates. Compression is positive. The app applies \(\phi_c\) to concrete and \(\phi_s\) to steel.

Equation 4 — requested P-at-moments ratio\[U_{PMM}=\frac{P_f}{P_r(M_{xf},M_{yf})}\]

The corresponding axial resistance is interpolated from the calculated interaction surface at the input moment vector. For tension or difficult non-monotonic branches, the app also evaluates the nearest radial surface intersection and reports the governing ratio.

Equation 5 — screening shear/torsion interaction\[U_{VT}=\frac{|V_y|}{V_{ry}}+\frac{|V_z|}{V_{rz}}+\frac{|T|}{T_r}\]

Shear resistance combines a simplified concrete term and tie contribution; torsion resistance uses a thin-walled closed-hoop analogy. This is deliberately identified as a screening check—not a substitute for the governing code’s full biaxial shear, torsion, crushing, minimum reinforcement, and detailing provisions.

2. Coding Theory

The application creates a perimeter bar model, numerically integrates concrete over a 28×28 cell grid, sweeps neutral-axis angles at 1° increments and calculates/stores Pr, Mxr, Myr, and Mr for every integer millimetre of neutral-axis depth c. Plotly renders the 3D surface and both requested contour families. Auto-design enumerates bar size and horizontal/vertical face counts, then retains the least-area passing candidate.
